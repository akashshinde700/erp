   109→### CB-R1 — `nextDocNumber` / `docPrefix()` connection-pool timeout
   110→- **Problem:** `docPrefix()` (and the first-number `createMany`) in `nextDocNumber` read through the global
   111→  Prisma client inside an interactive transaction, so each transaction needs a second pool connection. Bursts of
   112→  concurrent documents the size of the pool time out with P2024 in any module (seen with 5 connections in tests).
   113→- **Files:** `src/lib/erp-helpers.ts` (`docPrefix`, `nextDocNumber`); every route that numbers documents.
   114→- **Acceptance:** numbering inside a transaction uses only that transaction's connection, or the prefix is
   115→  resolved before the transaction; numbers stay unique and gap-free under concurrency; no P2024.
   116→- **Tests:** 2× pool-size concurrent first documents of a new financial year → all succeed, distinct sequential
   117→  numbers; existing numbering and import-concurrency tests unchanged.
   118→
   119→### CB-R2 — Same-day cheque bounce changes historical statements
   120→- **Problem:** a non-post-dated cheque counts on its payment date; when it bounces later it drops out from that
   121→  date, so statements and closing balances already reported change.
   122→- **Files:** `src/lib/accounts.ts` (`moneyPaymentWhere`), `src/app/api/payments/[id]/route.ts` (bounce),
   123→  `src/app/api/accounts/[id]/statement/route.ts`, `src/lib/accounting.ts` (cash flow).
   124→- **Acceptance:** the original receipt stays on its date; the bounce is a separate outflow on the bounce date;
   125→  balances for dates before the bounce do not change; the invoice reopens as today.
   126→- **Tests:** balance as of the payment day is the same before and after the bounce; balance after the bounce date
   127→  is reduced; the statement shows both rows; cash flow `difference` stays 0.
   128→
   129→### CB-R3 — IST normalisation of invoice and bill dates
   130→- **Problem:** sales invoice and purchase bill documents keep `new Date(body.date)` / `new Date()`; only their
   131→  payment rows are normalised, so a document saved between 00:00 and 05:30 IST can fall outside its day in reports.
   132→- **Files:** `src/app/api/sales-invoices/route.ts`, `src/app/api/purchase-invoices/route.ts` (+ their `[id]` edit routes).
   133→- **Acceptance:** document dates use `businessDate()`; the invoice and its "received / paid now" payment carry
   134→  the same IST day; GST and P&L reports for that day include the document.
   135→- **Tests:** invoice created at 00:30 IST with no date → stored as that IST day, in that day's sales report and
   136→  GST totals; date-only input unchanged.
   137→
   138→### CB-R4 — Daybook shows post-dated cheque money on the payment date
   139→- **Problem:** `listTransactions` (daybook / transactions report) puts a cleared post-dated cheque's money on the
   140→  payment date, while balances and cash flow use the clearing date.
   141→- **Files:** `src/lib/accounting.ts` (`listTransactions`), transactions/daybook report route and UI.
   142→- **Acceptance:** money in/out of a cleared post-dated cheque appears on its clearing date; the payment-date row
   143→  shows the settlement with no money; daybook money totals for a period equal the cash-flow movement.
   144→- **Tests:** post-dated cheque recorded on day A and cleared on day B → daybook money on B only; period totals
   145→  match `cashFlow()`.
   146→
   147→### CB-R5 — Payment mode vs account type mismatch
   148→- **Problem:** an explicitly chosen account is not checked against the mode (e.g. CASH into a bank account,

   150→- **Files:** `src/lib/accounts.ts` (`postingAccount`), payments, expenses, sales/purchase invoice routes; forms

   152→- **Acceptance:** CASH ↔ cash accounts, other modes ↔ bank accounts, with a clear 400 message; forms offer only
   153→  matching accounts. Decide before building whether UPI into a cash account is ever allowed.
   154→- **Tests:** each mismatch refused on every route; matching pairs accepted; existing records untouched.

   374→## AC-B1 fix — first attempt STOPPED (2026-10-25): original cost is not recorded (resolved below)
   375→Approved scope was "restore returned goods at the original cost recorded in that sale's stock records". Tracing shows
   376→that cost does not exist in the records, so — as instructed — no fallback was invented and no app code was changed.
   377→
   378→Verified facts:
   379→- Costing is **weighted average per item + warehouse** (`postStock`, `src/lib/erp-helpers.ts`): `StockBalance.avgRate`
   380→  changes only on stock-in rows with a rate; outward rows do not change it.
   381→- The sale's outward stock rows store the **selling price**, not cost: direct invoice `SALE_OUT` uses `rate: l.rate`
   382→  (`sales-invoices/shared.ts` `postSaleStock`); challan `DISPATCH_OUT` uses the SO rate or the item's selling price
   383→  (`deliveries/[id]/route.ts:116`). Cost at the moment of sale is not stored anywhere.
   384→- Batches (`ItemBatch`) carry no cost. Reports value stock as quantity × current `avgRate`.
   385→- Invoice cancel already re-enters stock at the **current** average (`SALE_CANCEL_IN`, `rate = avgRate`).
   386→- Related gap found: a return against an invoice that never moved stock (direct invoice with "deduct stock" off) still
   387→  adds stock — goods that never left are counted in again (inventory double count).
   388→- Outward `rate` is shown by the stock ledger / item-detail screens, so re-using it to hold cost would change what
   389→  those screens show.

   392→| Option | What the return uses | Schema | Notes |

   394→| A | Weighted-average cost **at the moment of the original sale**, rebuilt by replaying that item + warehouse's ledger up to the sale's outward row | none | Closest to "original cost"; deterministic; depends on ledger order; legacy wrong returns are replayed as they were |
   395→| B | **Current** weighted-average cost of the return warehouse at the return date | none | Same rule as invoice cancel; simple; not the original cost |
   396→| C | Store unit cost on outward rows from now on (new column); returns use it; older sales fall back to A or B | **additive schema change** | Cleanest long term; needs schema approval and the deploy gate |

   702→**Known limitations / risks:**
   703→1. **Behaviour change for users:** cash outflows that take cash below zero are refused (only a Tenant Admin can save
   704→   them); repeated same-day entries ask for confirmation; roles without `cash-bank` no longer see balances in the
   705→   account picker; sales / purchase invoice lists need the module (or receivables / payables / GST); production /
   706→   plant / quality managers lose the financial report cards. Existing negative cash balances (legacy) make any new
   707→   cash outflow dated on/after the negative day fail until fixed or overridden.
   708→2. **Duplicate check is advisory and not race-proof:** two identical requests sent at the same moment with
   709→   different keys can both pass it (the idempotency key and the client's in-flight de-duplication cover retries and
   710→   double clicks, not two separate devices).
   711→3. **READ COMMITTED** is used for the guarded money transactions (and purchase bills with "paid now"); other
   712→   transactions keep the default. Row-lock order is numbering counter → cash account → idempotency row; deadlocks
   713→   surface as 503 "server was busy — try again".
   714→4. **Idempotency**: a request whose process died keeps its key "in progress" for up to 10 minutes; the stored
   715→   response is the original JSON (later edits to the document are not reflected in a replay); edits, deletes and
   716→   cheque actions are not keyed (they are protected by conditional updates instead).
   717→5. Not gated (outside the approved scope; no balances): `parties/last-rates`, `stock-ledger`.
   718→6. Opening-balance date moved later and cheque clearing-date edits are not checked by the guard (no cash effect
   719→   possible: entries before a new opening date are refused; cheques are bank-only since CB-R5).
   720→7. `MANAGEMENT` sees everything (`'*'`) but cannot override negative cash, by decision.
